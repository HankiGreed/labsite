01

Principal Investigator

Dr. Tushar Shaw

Assistant Professor | Principal Investigator

I am Dr. Tushar Shaw, an Assistant Professor at M.S. Ramaiah University of Applied Sciences (RUAS), Bengaluru, with a Ph.D. in Microbiology from Manipal Academy of Higher Education (MAHE).

My research focuses on infectious diseases, antimicrobial resistance, bacterial genomics, molecular epidemiology, and diagnostic innovation. I am particularly interested in translating microbiological research into practical diagnostic solutions that address important challenges in infectious disease detection and management.

Beyond research, I am passionate about teaching, mentoring young researchers, and fostering interdisciplinary scientific collaborations. I enjoy reading, travelling, and experimenting with new recipes in the kitchen.

I welcome opportunities to connect with fellow researchers, exchange scientific ideas, and explore collaborations that advance infectious disease research and diagnostic innovations.

Let’s connect, learn, and grow together!

02

Researchers and Doctoral Scholars

Vaishnavi Vishram Naik

PhD Research Scholar | Infectious Diseases and Antimicrobial Resistance

Vaishnavi holds an MSc in Medical Microbiology from Kasturba Medical College (KMC). Her research interests include infectious diseases, antimicrobial resistance, Streptococcus pneumoniae, whole-genome sequencing, and metagenomics. She is also interested in developing antimicrobial peptide-based therapeutics for infectious diseases. Outside the laboratory, she enjoys reading crime thrillers and exploring mysteries.

Anu Kurian

PhD Research Scholar | Assistant Professor of Microbiology | Oral Microbiome and Molecular Diagnostics

Anu has over 14 years of teaching and research experience, with interests in medical and public health microbiology, oral microbiome, and microbial dysbiosis. Her doctoral research focuses on risk factors and oral microbial communities associated with early childhood caries in Bengaluru. Her research integrates Cariogram-based risk assessment, Illumina metagenomic sequencing, PCR, and microbiome analysis.

Guruprasad Baipadithaya

Project Research Scientist-I | Infectious Disease Diagnostics

Guruprasad has over 9 years of research experience in antibody development, recombinant protein technology, and molecular biology. His current research focuses on developing and characterizing antibodies for lateral flow assays and rapid detection of bacterial pathogens. His expertise includes hybridoma technology, immunoassays, recombinant protein expression, and diagnostic assay development. Outside the laboratory, Guruprasad enjoys pursuing his entrepreneurial interests alongside his scientific career.

Ramnadh Yenduri

Project Technical Staff | Antimicrobial Resistance Diagnostics

Ramnadh holds a B.Tech in Biotechnology and a Master’s degree in Cell and Molecular Biology from Uppsala University. His current work is focused on developing bioimaging-based diagnostic devices for antimicrobial resistance in Gram-negative bacteria causing urinary tract infections. His expertise spans clinical microbiology, molecular biology, protein biochemistry, and structural biology. Outside the laboratory, Ramnadh is a sports enthusiast who enjoys watching Formula 1 and football, along with reading psychological thriller novels.
