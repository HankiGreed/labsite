Research

01

Mapping bacterial carriage, transmission, and population diversity.

Investigating bacterial carriage, transmission, population structure, serotype distribution, genetic diversity, and virulence-associated characteristics using molecular epidemiology and genomic approaches.

02

Tracking resistance-associated genetic determinants and their rapid detection.

Characterizing antimicrobial-resistant bacteria, investigating resistance-associated genetic determinants, and developing molecular approaches for the rapid detection of clinically important resistance mechanisms.

03

Linking respiratory carriage surveillance with diagnostics for childhood pneumonia.

Studying respiratory pathogens, particularly Streptococcus pneumoniae and Haemophilus influenzae, to improve pathogen detection, carriage surveillance, and the development of diagnostics for community-acquired pneumonia.

04

Building accessible PCR, LAMP, and lateral flow technologies for pathogen detection.

Developing accessible diagnostic technologies, including multiplex PCR, real-time PCR, LAMP, and lateral flow assays, for rapid identification of bacterial pathogens and selected antimicrobial resistance determinants.

05

Developing pathogen-targeted antibodies and immunodiagnostics, including for melioidosis.

Exploring pathogen-associated proteins, antibody development, and immunodiagnostic technologies for rapid detection of clinically important bacterial infections, including melioidosis.

06

Exploring peptide-based bacterial control for persistent endodontic infections.

Investigating peptide-based approaches for bacterial control and endodontic disinfection, with emphasis on antimicrobial activity and potential applications in persistent polymicrobial infections.

Tushar Shaw Lab

Tushar Shaw Lab, led by Dr. Tushar Shaw at M.S. Ramaiah University of Applied Sciences (RUAS), Bengaluru, India, focuses on understanding infectious diseases, investigating bacterial pathogens, and developing rapid, reliable, and cost-effective diagnostic technologies.
