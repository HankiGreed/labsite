01 / About the laboratory

Infectious disease research

Tushar Shaw’s Lab, led by Dr. Tushar Shaw at M.S. Ramaiah University of Applied Sciences (RUAS), Bengaluru, India, focuses on understanding infectious diseases, investigating bacterial pathogens, and developing rapid, reliable, and cost-effective diagnostic technologies.

The laboratory integrates clinical microbiology, molecular biology, molecular epidemiology, genomics, and bioinformatics to address key challenges in infectious disease diagnosis and antimicrobial resistance. Its research interests include bacterial carriage and transmission, pathogen diversity, virulence, and antimicrobial resistance, with an emphasis on respiratory pathogens, Gram-negative bacteria, and emerging infectious diseases.

03 / Research at a glance

Research areas

01

Bacterial Epidemiology and Genomics

Mapping bacterial carriage, transmission, and population diversity.

02

Antimicrobial Resistance and Molecular Surveillance

Tracking resistance-associated genetic determinants and their rapid detection.

03

Respiratory Pathogens and Paediatric Pneumonia

Linking respiratory carriage surveillance with diagnostics for childhood pneumonia.

04

Rapid Diagnostics

Building accessible PCR, LAMP, and lateral flow technologies for pathogen detection.

05

Antibody-Based Diagnostics and Emerging Infections

Developing pathogen-targeted antibodies and immunodiagnostics, including for melioidosis.

06

Antimicrobial Peptides and Infection Control

Exploring peptide-based bacterial control for persistent endodontic infections.
