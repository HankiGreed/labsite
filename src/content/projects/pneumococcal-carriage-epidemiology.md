### Project title

Nasopharyngeal Carriage of _Streptococcus pneumoniae_ Serotypes Among Children and Their Cohabiting Relatives in South India: A Cross-Sectional Study

### Background and rationale

Nasopharyngeal carriage of _Streptococcus pneumoniae_ is an important component of pneumococcal transmission within households and communities. Children and their cohabiting family members may contribute to the circulation of pneumococcal strains, including serotypes associated with disease. Understanding carriage patterns and serotype distribution can provide valuable epidemiological information for public health surveillance.

### Project objectives

- Determine the occurrence of pneumococcal carriage among children and their cohabiting relatives.
- Characterize the distribution of pneumococcal serotypes in the study population.
- Investigate patterns of carriage within households and explore relevant epidemiological associations.

### Scientific significance

The study aims to improve understanding of pneumococcal carriage and household transmission dynamics in South India. Its findings can contribute to regional pneumococcal epidemiology and inform future surveillance and prevention strategies.
