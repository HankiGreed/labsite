### Project title

Development and Evaluation of an Economical Loop-Mediated Isothermal Amplification Combined with a Lateral Flow Assay for Rapid Detection of Carbapenem-Resistant Enterobacteriaceae

### Background and rationale

Carbapenem resistance among clinically important Gram-negative bacteria presents a major challenge for infection management. Conventional antimicrobial susceptibility testing and molecular methods can involve considerable infrastructure, time, and cost. Combining isothermal nucleic acid amplification with lateral flow detection offers a potential approach for simplifying the detection of selected resistance determinants.

### Project objectives

- Develop an economical LAMP-based approach for detecting selected carbapenem resistance-associated genetic targets.
- Integrate amplification with lateral flow-based result visualization.
- Evaluate analytical performance and diagnostic feasibility using appropriate reference methods and study specimens.

### Scientific significance

The project explores a potentially accessible molecular diagnostic strategy for carbapenem resistance detection. The approach may help support timely laboratory decision-making, particularly in settings where rapid and cost-conscious diagnostic technologies are needed.
