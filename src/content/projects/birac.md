### Project title

Development of Monoclonal Antibody-Based Point-of-Care Test Targeting _Burkholderia pseudomallei_ Membrane Proteins for Rapid Detection of Melioidosis

### Background and rationale

Melioidosis is an infectious disease caused by _Burkholderia pseudomallei_. Timely diagnosis can be challenging because its clinical presentation may overlap with other infections, while conventional diagnostic workflows may require specialized laboratory resources. Antibody-based point-of-care technologies offer a potential route towards rapid detection of pathogen-associated targets.

### Project objectives

- Identify and develop suitable monoclonal antibodies against selected _B. pseudomallei_ membrane-associated protein targets.
- Develop an antibody-based point-of-care diagnostic format for detecting melioidosis-associated targets.
- Evaluate analytical performance, target specificity, and diagnostic feasibility using appropriate reference methods and specimens.

### Scientific significance

The project aims to translate pathogen-targeted antibody technology into a rapid diagnostic platform for melioidosis. Successful development and validation could contribute to improved access to timely diagnosis and support clinical decision-making in settings where melioidosis is an important public health concern.
