Dr. Tushar Shaw presented findings from an Indian Council of Medical Research (ICMR)-funded research project on Streptococcus pneumoniae carriage at ESCMID Global, Barcelona, Spain.

His participation was supported by a travel grant from Förderverein, facilitating the international dissemination of research findings in infectious diseases and pneumococcal epidemiology.
