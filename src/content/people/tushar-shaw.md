I am Dr. Tushar Shaw, an Assistant Professor at M.S. Ramaiah University of Applied Sciences (RUAS), Bengaluru, with a Ph.D. in Microbiology from Manipal Academy of Higher Education (MAHE).

My research focuses on infectious diseases, antimicrobial resistance, bacterial genomics, molecular epidemiology, and diagnostic innovation. I am particularly interested in translating microbiological research into practical diagnostic solutions that address important challenges in infectious disease detection and management.

Beyond research, I am passionate about teaching, mentoring young researchers, and fostering interdisciplinary scientific collaborations. I enjoy reading, travelling, and experimenting with new recipes in the kitchen.

I welcome opportunities to connect with fellow researchers, exchange scientific ideas, and explore collaborations that advance infectious disease research and diagnostic innovations.

**Let’s connect, learn, and grow together!**
