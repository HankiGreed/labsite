Vaishnavi holds an MSc in Medical Microbiology from Kasturba Medical College (KMC). Her research interests include infectious diseases, antimicrobial resistance, _Streptococcus pneumoniae_, whole-genome sequencing, and metagenomics. She is also interested in developing antimicrobial peptide-based therapeutics for infectious diseases. Outside the laboratory, she enjoys reading crime thrillers and exploring mysteries.
