Guruprasad has over 9 years of research experience in antibody development, recombinant protein technology, and molecular biology. His current research focuses on developing and characterizing antibodies for lateral flow assays and rapid detection of bacterial pathogens. His expertise includes hybridoma technology, immunoassays, recombinant protein expression, and diagnostic assay development. Outside the laboratory, Guruprasad enjoys pursuing his entrepreneurial interests alongside his scientific career.
