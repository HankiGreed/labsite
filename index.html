Infectious diseases, bacterial pathogens, and rapid diagnostics

Tushar Shaw Lab, led by Dr. Tushar Shaw at M.S. Ramaiah University of Applied Sciences (RUAS), Bengaluru, India, focuses on understanding infectious diseases, investigating bacterial pathogens, and developing rapid, reliable, and cost-effective diagnostic technologies.

01 / Research

Research at a glance

Four research themes connect pathogen biology, antimicrobial resistance, and rapid diagnostic development.

01

Molecular Epidemiology and Genomics

Characterizing bacterial genetic diversity, population structure, serotype distribution, and virulence-associated traits using molecular typing and whole-genome sequencing, with a particular focus on Streptococcus pneumoniae.

Explore theme ↗

02

Antimicrobial Resistance Surveillance and Detection

Investigating antimicrobial resistance mechanisms in clinically important bacteria, particularly carbapenem resistance in Gram-negative pathogens, and developing molecular approaches for rapid detection of antimicrobial resistance determinants.

Explore theme ↗

03

Rapid Molecular Diagnostics

Developing and evaluating multiplex PCR, real-time PCR, loop-mediated isothermal amplification (LAMP), and lateral flow assays for rapid detection of bacterial pathogens and antimicrobial resistance genes.

Explore theme ↗

04

Respiratory Pathogen Research

Investigating Streptococcus pneumoniae, Haemophilus influenzae, and atypical pneumonia-associated pathogens, with emphasis on pathogen detection, epidemiological surveillance, and the development of diagnostic approaches for community-acquired pneumonia in children.
