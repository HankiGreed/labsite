International Conference Presentation & Travel Grant — ESCMID Global, Barcelona, Spain

Dr. Tushar Shaw | ESCMID Global, Barcelona, Spain

2024

Resource Person — MICROCON 2024

Dr. Tushar Shaw | Bharatratna Atal Bihari Vajpayee Medical College, Pune

Dr. Tushar Shaw served as a resource person at MICROCON 2024, contributing to a hands-on workshop titled “Melioidosis: Hands-on Workshop for Appropriate and Safe Diagnosis in the Laboratory.” The workshop focused on laboratory diagnosis of melioidosis, emphasizing appropriate diagnostic practices and biosafety considerations for the safe handling and identification of Burkholderia pseudomallei.

Faculty Development Programme — Invited Speaker

Dr. Tushar Shaw | Acharya Institute of Allied Health Sciences

Dr. Tushar Shaw delivered a Faculty Development Programme (FDP) session at Acharya Institute of Allied Health Sciences on “Choosing a Research Topic and the Importance of Literature Review in Research.” The session focused on identifying relevant research questions, selecting appropriate research topics, and understanding the role of a comprehensive literature review in developing a strong research foundation.

Invited Guest Lecture — Clinicogenomics in Infectious Disease Diagnostics

Dr. Tushar Shaw | Department of Microbiology, Mount Carmel College, Bengaluru

Dr. Tushar Shaw delivered an invited academic session for undergraduate students of the Department of Microbiology at Mount Carmel College, Bengaluru, titled “Clinicogenomics in Infectious Disease Diagnostics: Leap from Research Laboratory to the Clinic.” The session explored the integration of genomic approaches into infectious disease diagnosis and highlighted the translation of laboratory research into clinically relevant diagnostic applications.

Invited Session on Research Grant Writing — RUAS

Dr. Tushar Shaw | Office of Research, M. S. Ramaiah University of Applied Sciences

Dr. Tushar Shaw was invited to share his experiences in research grant writing with faculty members from various disciplines at RUAS. His session focused on challenges encountered during grant applications, common reasons for unsuccessful proposals, lessons learned from grant rejections, and strategies for developing stronger and more competitive research proposals.

Panelist — ASM Student Chapter, RUAS

Dr. Tushar Shaw | Faculty of Life and Allied Health Sciences, M. S. Ramaiah University of Applied Sciences

Dr. Tushar Shaw participated as a panelist in an academic discussion organized by the American Society for Microbiology (ASM) Student Chapter at RUAS. The discussion, titled “Between Clinical Complexities and the Importance of Rapid Diagnostics in Clinical Microbiology,” explored the challenges of clinical diagnosis and the role of rapid diagnostic approaches in improving pathogen detection and clinical decision-making.
