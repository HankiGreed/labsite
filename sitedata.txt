About the laboratory
Tushar Shaw’s Lab, led by Dr. Tushar Shaw at M.S. Ramaiah University of Applied Sciences
(RUAS), Bengaluru, India, focuses on understanding infectious diseases, investigating bacterial
pathogens, and developing rapid, reliable, and cost-effective diagnostic technologies.
The laboratory integrates clinical microbiology, molecular biology, molecular epidemiology,
genomics, and bioinformatics to address key challenges in infectious disease diagnosis and
antimicrobial resistance. Its research interests include bacterial carriage and transmission,
pathogen diversity, virulence, and antimicrobial resistance, with an emphasis on respiratory
pathogens, Gram-negative bacteria, and emerging infectious diseases.
Research at a glance
Molecular Epidemiology and Genomics
Characterizing bacterial genetic diversity, population structure, serotype distribution, and
virulence-associated traits using molecular typing and whole-genome sequencing, with a
particular focus on Streptococcus pneumoniae.
Antimicrobial Resistance Surveillance and Detection
Investigating antimicrobial resistance mechanisms in clinically important bacteria, particularly
carbapenem resistance in Gram-negative pathogens, and developing molecular approaches for
rapid detection of antimicrobial resistance determinants.
Rapid Molecular Diagnostics
Developing and evaluating multiplex PCR, real-time PCR, loop-mediated isothermal
amplification (LAMP), and lateral flow assays for rapid detection of bacterial pathogens and
antimicrobial resistance genes.
Respiratory Pathogen Research
Investigating Streptococcus pneumoniae, Haemophilus influenzae, and atypical pneumoniaassociated pathogens, with emphasis on pathogen detection, epidemiological surveillance, and
the development of diagnostic approaches for community-acquired pneumonia in children.
Pathogens of interest
Respiratory Pathogens
• Streptococcus pneumoniae — carriage, serotyping, genomics and epidemiology.
• Haemophilus influenzae — pathogen detection and respiratory diagnostics.
Antimicrobial-Resistant Bacteria
• Acinetobacter baumannii — genomic diversity and antimicrobial resistance.
• Escherichia coli — pathogen characterization and resistance surveillance.
• Klebsiella pneumoniae — antimicrobial resistance and pathogen characterization.
• Carbapenem-resistant Enterobacterales (CRE) — rapid detection of carbapenem
resistance.
Hypervirulent Pathogens
- Hypervirulent Klebsiella pneumoniae (hvKp) — virulence-associated characteristics and
pathogen detection.
Endodontic and Other Bacterial Pathogens
• Enterococcus faecalis& S.mutans — pathogenicity, antimicrobial susceptibility and
persistent endodontic infections.
• Anaerobic bacteria — pathogens associated with polymicrobial infections.
Fungal Pathogens
- Candida albicans — fungal pathogenicity and infection-related research.
